Fruit Ninja - Hand Edition. Open index.html in Chrome/Edge.
- "Play now": allow camera, point your index finger, swipe fast. Needs internet (hand-tracking model + fonts) and https:// or localhost or file://.
- "Play with mouse instead": swipe the mouse fast over fruit. Works offline.
- Settings (gear icon): sensitivity, sound, visual effects (High/Low). Esc / P = pause.
